Andromeda Car Racing – Privacy Policy

Developer: JerlinPhoenix · Effective 25 November 2026

Andromeda Car Racing is a racing game that works fully offline. It does not collect, send or share any personal information.

The game itself is made by JerlinPhoenix. On Android and iPhone it is installed and updated through the app store (Google Play or the App Store), under that store's own privacy policy.

What the game stores

Your progress (coins, high scores, best lap times, cars you own, upgrades, missions) and your settings (sound, controls, graphics) are saved only on your own device. They never leave it. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data, deletes them.

What the game does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers.
  • No advertisements.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
  • The game sends nothing about you over the internet. Its cars, scenery and sounds are all inside the app. The only thing it fetches is a small file saying which version is the latest (see Updates below).

Suggestions and crash reports

The game keeps a small log on your device: what it was doing, how smoothly it was running, your device model, and any error. Lines older than 7 days are deleted, and the log is kept under about 200 KB. It holds no name, account or location, and it stays on your device.

If the game hits an error it asks whether you want to send a report. If you choose to, or if you send a suggestion from Settings, the game opens your own mail app with the message already written, addressed to the game's support address, andromeda.racing.support@gmail.com. You can read it there, and it is sent only if you press Send in your mail app. If you send it, we receive your email address along with the message, as with any email, and use it only to reply. If you press Cancel, nothing is sent.

Updates

When the game starts, and when you press Check for updates in Settings, it downloads a small file (version.json) from the game's website, toniomathio.github.io/car-racing, to see whether a newer version is out. The request carries nothing about you, your device or your progress. Like any visit to a website, the site's host, GitHub, sees your device's internet (IP) address; see GitHub's privacy statement. Without a connection the game simply does not check. If you choose to update, the game opens Google Play, the App Store or the game's website.

Backing up your progress

On a phone or tablet the game asks, the first time it starts, whether your progress (coins, vehicles, scores) should be included in your device's own backup: your Google account on Android, iCloud on an iPhone or iPad. If you say yes, the game keeps a copy of your progress on the device where that backup picks it up, so it comes back when you install the game again. The backup is made by your device, under your device's settings and Google's or Apple's privacy policy. We never receive it. If you say no, nothing is copied. You can change your answer in Settings.

You can also save your progress as a file from Settings and keep it wherever you like. The file goes only where you put it.

Coin packs

Coin packs are not on sale in this version. If they return: buying a coin pack is optional. It is paid for through the app store (Google Play or the App Store), which handles the payment under its own privacy policy. The game never sees your payment details; it only learns from the store that the purchase went through, and keeps a record of it on your device so the coins are added once.

Device features the game uses

  • Motion sensor: only if you choose tilt steering, to read how you tilt the device. The readings are used for steering at that moment and are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Vibration: a short buzz on crashes and pickups, which you can switch off in Settings.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this (for example by adding advertisements), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
