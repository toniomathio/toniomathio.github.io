🧩Classic block puzzle

Fit blocks on a 10×10 board. Fill rows and columns to clear them — clear several at once and chain combos for big scores.

🎮Five ways to play

Endless Classic, a two-minute Timed mode, a Daily Challenge, 12 Challenges and 100 Levels with stars.

📴Works offline

No internet needed to play. No sign-up, no account — your progress saves automatically on your device.

💾Take your progress along

Export a backup file and import it on a new phone or tablet to keep your coins, scores and unlocks.

🎨Make it yours

Earn coins to unlock themes like Wood, Neon and Ocean, plus new block styles and boards.

🏆Goals every day

Daily rewards, daily and weekly missions and 30 achievements to collect.
