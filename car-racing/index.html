Andromeda Car Racing

A 3D racing game for your phone, tablet and computer. Race on highways and circuits against computer drivers, through traffic, day and night, rain or shine. It works fully offline.

On the road

Highway races, circuits, nitro, a garage full of cars, bikes and trucks, and the view from the driver's seat.

What's in it

Four ways to race, and every vehicle drives by its own figures.

Four modes

Endless for a high score, Free Ride as long as you like, a 6 km Highway Race against five rivals, and Circuit racing over laps against seven.

100 circuits

Parkland, desert, city streets, snow, coast and jungle tracks. The first five are open; the rest open as your Racer Rating climbs.

30 vehicles

Cars, motorcycles, trucks and off-road vehicles from six made-up makers, each with engine, handling and nitro upgrades and paint.

Real traffic

Traffic signals before changing lane, follows the car ahead and moves over when you sound the horn.

Your controls

Tilt, buttons, a steering wheel or the sides of the screen, with steering and brake help, left-handed layouts and a cockpit view.

Daily goals

Three goals a day, the same for everyone, plus a bonus. Level up, earn coins and climb from Rookie to Legend.

Play anywhere

One game for phones, tablets and computers. Your progress stays on your device, and you can keep a backup file of it.
