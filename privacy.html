Privacy Policy

Andromeda Block Puzzle · Developer: JerlinPhoenix · Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Andromeda Block Puzzle (Android, iOS and web) handles. The short version: we do not collect, store or sell any of your personal data. There are no accounts and no game server. Ads in the game are provided by Google, as described below.

1. Who we are

The game is developed by JerlinPhoenix. Contact: toniomathio@gmail.com.

2. Your game progress stays on your device

Scores, coins, unlocked themes, achievements, settings and your current game are saved only on your device (in the app's private storage, or your browser's storage for the web version). We have no server, so this information is never sent to us.

On Android, the system's own backup (your Google account's device backup) may include the game's save, so progress can come back after a reinstall. This is controlled by your device settings, not by us.

3. Backup files

A backup file is created only when you choose Export or Share in the game. It contains your game progress and settings and goes wherever you decide to save or send it. We never receive a copy.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob / AdSense)

The game is free and supported by ads from Google: Google AdMob in the Android and iOS apps and Google AdSense on the web. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google and its partners may collect and process information such as:

DataPurpose
Device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, Apple IDFA where permitted)Ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement and — only with your consent — personalised ads
IP address and approximate location derived from itChoosing ads relevant to your region, fraud prevention
Device and app information (model, OS version, language, ad interactions)Ad delivery, measurement, diagnostics

This data is collected by Google under Google's privacy policy, not by us. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

5. Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): before any ad is shown, the game displays Google's consent message. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings › Ad privacy choices.
  • iPhone / iPad: the game asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are still shown but not personalised using your device identifier. You can change this in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • You can manage ad personalisation for your Google account at myadcenter.google.com.

6. Update check

About once a day, when you are online, the Android and iOS apps ask Google Play or the App Store whether a newer version exists, and download a small file (version.json) from this website, hosted by GitHub Pages. No personal information, game progress or identifiers are sent by us; like any web request, the store and GitHub see your IP address. You can always choose to update later.

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Deleting your data

Because your progress is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time with Settings › Reset progress in the game, by clearing the app's (or website's) data, or by uninstalling the app. We hold no copy that we could delete for you.

9. Security

Saved data is kept in your device's app-private storage. Backup files contain only game progress; keep them somewhere safe if you want to restore them later.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: toniomathio@gmail.com
